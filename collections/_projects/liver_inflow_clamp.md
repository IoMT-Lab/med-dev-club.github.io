Robotic liver surgery requires intermittent clamping of the inflow vasculature, but requires frequent and disruptive repositioning of the tools and operating room personnel, and ultimately increases long-term liver damage due to extended periods of ligation.
Create a disposable, low-profile, device that can be deployed through an abdominal trocar and controlled remotely.
