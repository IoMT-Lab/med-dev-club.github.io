Melanoma patients with a positive sentinel lymph node are often monitored with regular ultrasound scans instead of undergoing complete lymph node removal. However, scan quality and interpretation can vary widely depending on the equipment, technician, and radiologist, particularly in rural or satellite hospitals. This project aims to develop imaging and analysis methods that provide more consistent and accurate results, helping detect cancerous lymph nodes earlier, reduce unnecessary travel and treatment costs, and improve access to specialized care.
