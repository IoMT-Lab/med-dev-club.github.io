Surgeons often have a need to deliver a precise dose of medication to a very specific location.
Commercially available cathethers often have a predefined size and/or pattern of fenestrations that may not align with the needs of a particular operation.
Design and evaluate simpler options of catheter modification to facilitate updates by surgeon and/or staff during ongoing procedure.
