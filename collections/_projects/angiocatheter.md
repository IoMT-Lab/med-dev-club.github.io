Patients whose vessels are unusually small or fragile, a condition most frequently encountered at the extremes of age (elderly patients and pediatric or neonatal patients) tend to be difficult intravenous sticks. An expandable catheter that would allow for easier placement yet allow for higher flow rates once inside the vessel could improve the patient experience, increase treatment options and potentially consolidate SKU numbers that are managed and stocked.
